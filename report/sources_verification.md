# Source verification and quality grades

Checked 2 Nov 2026. Every DOI was resolved through Crossref (or DataCite); abstracts were read through Europe PMC, PubMed or the publisher page.
**Full texts were NOT read.** Claims in the report are limited to what each abstract or page states.

## Grades
- **A**: peer-reviewed journal or conference paper; DOI resolves; abstract read; directly relevant.
- **B**: peer-reviewed; DOI resolves; used for method or background (abstract or title only).
- **C**: not peer reviewed (working paper or preprint); flagged in the report.
- **D**: official web page or documentation; used for factual statements only.

## Corrections made after the independent review (2 Nov 2026)
- Removed from the report to stay inside the word limit: Au-Yeung 2009 (no weather) and Klauber and Koch 2023 (working paper). The report now has 34 sources.
- Javari 2026: the AUC of 0.84 is for predicting prolonged hospital stay (over 7 days), not admissions. Report fixed.
- Weismann 2023: the 6% is "air quality and climate", not air pollution alone. Report fixed.
- Ogata 2021: the abstract says "non-linear multivariable predictors and/or under-sampling and bagging"; the "hybrid GAM + XGBoost" wording was removed.
- Sudarshan 2021: the abstract has no with/without-weather comparison, so the report no longer contrasts it with Marcilio.
- Garcia Crespi 2026: abstract says "southern Europe"; the report no longer says "Spanish".
- Data: weather pinned to ERA5-Land (Open-Meteo default blends models); attendance switched to the PHS "Unplanned" category (the "All" category includes planned attendances from Dec 2020). All preliminary numbers were re-run.

## Known limits
- The statement that the Heat-Health Alert service covers England only comes from the Met Office page as extracted. Confirm on the live page.
- The claim "no Scottish weekly weather-driven A&E forecast found" rests on focused searches, not a systematic review. The report says so.
- The Open-Meteo licence text itself could not be read directly. The non-commercial limit comes from the documentation pages.
- Read these in full before submitting or defending: Murtas, Vollmer, Marcilio, Blanco, Corcuera Hotz, Wan, Garcia Crespi.
