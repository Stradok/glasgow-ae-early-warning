## 6. Caveats

- Air quality is CAMS **modelled** output at a coarse grid, not monitor measurements.
- One weather point (city centre) is used for both hospitals.
- Counts are all attendances, not diagnoses.
- No machine-learning model has been trained yet; only baselines.
